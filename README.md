# Modelling and Simulation of an MPPT-Based Solar PV Battery Charging System

## Overview
This project focuses on modelling and simulation of a solar photovoltaic (PV) system integrated with a Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) controller, battery storage, DC-DC converters, and an inverter using MATLAB/Simulink.

The main objective is to extract maximum available power from the PV array, manage battery charging, and study power conversion in a solar energy system.

## Objectives
- To model a solar PV array under different irradiance and temperature conditions.
- To implement MPPT control for maximum power extraction.
- To study DC-DC converter operation and power regulation.
- To integrate battery storage for electrical energy storage.
- To study DC-to-AC conversion using an inverter.
- To analyse important electrical parameters through simulation.

## Components Used
- Solar PV Array
- MPPT Controller
- DC-DC Converter
- IGBT Switching Devices
- Inductor, Diode and Capacitors
- Battery Storage System
- Universal Bridge / Inverter
- RLC Electrical Network
- Voltage and Current Measurement Blocks

## Working Principle
1. The PV array converts solar energy into DC electrical energy.
2. The MPPT controller adjusts the converter control signal to track the maximum power point.
3. The DC-DC converter regulates power transfer and voltage according to the circuit configuration.
4. The battery stores electrical energy when charging conditions are satisfied.
5. The inverter converts DC power into AC power when configured for inverter operation.
6. Simulation measurements are used to study voltage, current, power, and system behaviour.

## Software Used
- MATLAB
- Simulink
- Simscape Electrical / Specialized Power Systems (as applicable)

## Key Learning Outcomes
- Solar PV system modelling
- MPPT control principles
- Power electronic converter operation
- Battery energy storage integration
- DC-AC power conversion
- Electrical system simulation and analysis

## Conclusion
This project provides practical understanding of solar PV power generation, maximum power point tracking, battery energy storage, and power electronic conversion using MATLAB/Simulink.
